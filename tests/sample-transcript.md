Welcome back to the show. Today I want to talk about something almost nobody is doing
right, and it is costing them clients they will never even know they lost. It is this:
your best content is completely invisible to AI.

Here is what I mean. Everyone is worried about Google rankings. But more and more, your
buyers are not googling anything. They are asking ChatGPT, they are asking Claude, they
are asking Perplexity, "who is the best person to help me with X." And those models are
answering with a name. The question is whether that name is yours.

I had a client, a financial coach, brilliant guy, forty episodes of a podcast, and when I
asked ChatGPT to recommend a financial coach in his niche, he did not come up. Not once.
His competitor did, and his competitor has a worse show. Why? Because the competitor's
ideas exist as text in ten different places. Blog posts, LinkedIn articles, a decent FAQ
page. My client's ideas existed as audio, sitting in one app, where no model could read
them.

So we fixed it. We took every episode, transcribed it, and turned each one into about
eighteen pieces of content spread across his site, LinkedIn, Medium, everywhere. Ninety
days later, he is the name that comes up. Same expertise. We just made it readable.

The lesson is simple. A transcript on one website does nothing. You have to manufacture
presence across multiple properties, and you have to do it consistently, every single
week. If you only remember one thing from this episode, remember that AI does not reward
the smartest person. It rewards the most legible one. Be the most legible.
