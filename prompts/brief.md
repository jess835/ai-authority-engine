(titles, articles, social posts, FAQ, and so on) will be generated from this brief plus
the transcript, so it must be accurate, specific, and grounded only in what was actually
said.

Client: {{CLIENT}}
Episode title: {{TITLE}}

TRANSCRIPT:
{{TRANSCRIPT}}

Produce the brief in exactly this structure, using Markdown headings:

## Core thesis
One or two sentences: the single biggest idea of this episode, in the client's framing.

## Who this is for
The specific listener or reader this episode serves, and the problem it solves for them.

## Key points
5 to 9 bullet points capturing the substantive arguments, steps, or insights, in the
order they were made. Each bullet is a full, standalone thought.

## Notable quotes
4 to 8 verbatim, word-for-word quotes from the transcript that are punchy, quotable, or
capture a core idea. Do not paraphrase. If a quote needs light trimming for length, use
an ellipsis, but never change the words.

## Topics and entities
The people, companies, products, tools, places, concepts, and proper nouns mentioned.
This feeds SEO, the entity map, and internal linking. Group as: People / Organisations /
Tools and products / Concepts.

## Hooks and angles
4 to 6 sharp, scroll-stopping angles or hooks this episode could be marketed with. These
seed the social posts and titles.

## Calls to action
Any offers, next steps, links, or asks the client made in the episode. If none, say "None
stated."

## Tone and voice notes
2 to 4 notes on how this client speaks, so downstream assets stay on-voice.
